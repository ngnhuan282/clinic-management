## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a clinical, trustworthy, and high-precision environment engineered for clinic management systems, electronic medical records (EMR), and patient scheduling workflows. The visual identity reflects absolute hygiene, professional authority, and compassionate calm. 

### Design Movement: Modern Corporate Healthcare
The system balances crisp utilitarian clarity with contemporary digital ergonomics. It avoids both cold stark sterility and playful consumer softness:
- **Calm & Clinical:** The tinted soft white backdrop reduces optic fatigue during long clinical shifts while establishing clear separation against pure white operational cards.
- **Data Precision:** Every layout choice serves high scannability, deterministic workflows, and high information density—crucial for doctors, triage nurses, and medical administrators.
- **Vietnamese Typographic Excellence:** Precise kerning and vertical metric balance tuned for diacritic-heavy medical terms, ICD-10 diagnostic entries, and pharmaceutical nomenclature.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around functional clinical blues, high-contrast structural text, and decisive semantic status signals.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary (`#1976D2`)**: The standard action color for primary CTAs (e.g., "Xác nhận lịch hẹn", "Kê đơn thuốc"), active stepper nodes, and primary navigation links.
- **Primary Dark (`#1565C0`) & Deep (`#0D47A1`)**: High-contrast states, navigation drawer headers, selected sidebar items, and hover actions on primary triggers.
- **Secondary (`#42A5F5`) & Secondary Light (`#90CAF9`)**: Timeline tracks, active slot indicators, subtle interactive accents, and focused field outlines.
- **Neutral Surface (`#F5F9FD`)**: The base canvas tint across the application. It diminishes monitor glare in high-stress clinic back-offices and guarantees clear contrast when pure `#FFFFFF` cards and panels are layered on top.
- **Neutrals & Text**:
  - `#1F2937` (Heading): Critical diagnostic headlines, doctor names, patient identities, and key metrics.
  - `#374151` (Body): Main body text, table cell values, and standard input values.
  - `#6B7280` (Muted): Secondary metadata, ICD-10 sub-labels, unit indicators (mg, ml, mmHg), and unselected state labels.

### Semantic Triage & Badge Palette
All status indicators must use dual-layer contrast (tinted background + saturated foreground token):
- **Success (`#10B981`, bg `#ECFDF5`)**: Confirmed appointments, paid invoices, cleared lab reports, verified check-ins.
- **Warning (`#F59E0B`, bg `#FFFBEB`)**: Pending approvals, triage queue wait-times, low pharmacy stock, follow-up required.
- **Danger (`#EF4444`, bg `#FEF2F2`)**: Clinical allergies, critical vitals out-of-range, canceled consultations, overdue payments.
- **Info (`#2563EB`, bg `#EFF6FF`)**: System notifications, doctor notes, upcoming scheduled visits.

## Typography

The type system relies on **Inter** to ensure flawless legibility of complex Vietnamese accents, medical symbols, and technical numeric strings across density configurations.

### Typographic Rules
- **Vietnamese Diacritic Integrity:** Line heights maintain a minimum ratio of 1.35x–1.5x on all text scales to eliminate accent clipping on tone-heavy Vietnamese words (e.g., `Khám Chuyên Khoa`, `Khoa Nội Tiết`, `Chẩn Đoán`).
- **Tabular Numerics:** All clinical vital metrics (blood pressure, heart rate, BMI), financial totals, and time slots must enforce `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to ensure exact column alignment in tables and appointment cards.
- **Code & Medical Classifications:** The `code-data` token applies to ICD-10 codes, medical record numbers (Mã hồ sơ), and prescription batch IDs with uppercase styling and slight tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an **8-point base grid** (with a secondary 4px unit for micro-alignments like chip internal padding and badge borders). It manages high information density without visual clutter.

### Layout Hierarchy
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column fluid grid. Max container width capped at 1440px for patient booking flows and full fluid 100% width with fixed 260px collapsible sidebar for administrative and doctor dashboards. Gutter: `24px` (`space-6`), Margin: `32px` (`space-8`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. Split views (e.g., Doctor selection on left, Slot matrix on right) stack or transition into collapsible sheets. Gutter: `16px` (`space-4`), Margin: `24px` (`space-6`).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Stepper switches from horizontal timeline to compact progress counter with current phase title. Gutter: `12px` (`space-3`), Margin: `16px` (`space-4`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined **tonal layering system** coupled with soft ambient micro-shadows tinted with clinical slate blue. Harsh dropshadows are explicitly avoided to preserve clean clinical sterility.

### Surface Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Filled with `#F5F9FD`. Flat, non-interactive foundation.
- **Level 1 (Clinical Cards & Table Rows):** `#FFFFFF` surface with a crisp structural hairline border `1px solid #E5E9F0`. Shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active/Hovered Card, Slot Selection):** `#FFFFFF` surface elevated with subtle blue-slate ambient shadow: `0px 4px 6px -1px rgba(25, 118, 210, 0.08), 0px 2px 4px -2px rgba(25, 118, 210, 0.04)`. Border shifts to `#90CAF9` or primary `#1976D2`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Slide-over EMR Drawers, Popovers):** `#FFFFFF` surface with deep diffused elevation: `0px 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0px 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry standard. Curves are sufficiently soft to feel human and approachable to anxious patients, while maintaining disciplined rectangular structure for medical data rows, calendar matrices, and formal prescriptions.

### Border Radius Tokens
- **`rounded-sm` (4px):** Checkboxes, table tags, status pills, small timeline nodes.
- **`rounded-base` (8px):** Form inputs, buttons, appointment time slot blocks, dropdown menus.
- **`rounded-lg` (12px):** Doctor profile cards, specialty selection tiles, dashboard metric widgets.
- **`rounded-xl` (16px):** Prescription preview modals, appointment summary containers, comprehensive stepper card frames.
- **`rounded-full` (9999px):** Avatars, step badge counters, notification count bubbles.

## Components

### 1. 6-Step Appointment Booking Stepper
- **Steps:** 1. Chọn Chuyên Khoa → 2. Bác Sĩ → 3. Ngày Khám → 4. Giờ Khám → 5. Lý Do Khám → 6. Xác Nhận.
- **Desktop Anatomy:** Horizontal progress rail. Completed steps feature a `#10B981` filled circle with a check icon; active step displays `#1976D2` with a pulsing ring (`0 0 0 4px #90CAF9`); future steps have a `#E5E9F0` neutral ring with `#6B7280` numbering.
- **Mobile Anatomy:** Sticky top bar displaying `Bước 3/6: Ngày Khám` with an inline 4px animated progress bar.

### 2. Time Slot Matrix (Chọn Giờ Khám)
- **Available Slot (`slot-available`):** Background `#F0FDF4`, border `1px solid #BBF7D0`, text `#166534`, hover background `#DCFCE7`. Displays start time in `tabular-nums` and a subtle green availability dot.
- **Occupied / Full Slot (`slot-occupied`):** Background `#F3F4F6`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, text `#9CA3AF`, pointer-events disabled, cross-hatch or "Đã kín" caption.
- **Selected Slot:** Solid primary fill `#1976D2`, text `#FFFFFF`, bold weight with drop checkmark.

### 3. Doctor & Specialty Profile Cards
- **Doctor Card:** Border `1px solid #E5E9F0`, 12px padding, horizontal split. Left side: 56px rounded avatar with medical degree badge ("BS.CKII"). Right side: Full name in `headline-sm`, specialty tag in `secondary-light` background, years of experience, rating, and earliest available consultation slot.
- **Specialty Card:** Icon container (44x44px, `#F5F9FD` background, `#1976D2` medical specialty iconography), specialty title, brief symptom guidance list, and active blue border upon selection.

### 4. Medical Data Tables (Bảng Dữ Liệu Y Tế)
- **Header:** Sticky header row, background `#F8FAFC`, uppercase `label-xs` text in `#6B7280`, 1px solid border at bottom.
- **Data Rows:** Height 52px (standard) / 40px (dense clinical mode), background `#FFFFFF`, hover row tint `#F5F9FD`.
- **Numeric & Code Cells:** Formatted with `tabular-nums` right-aligned for billing/vital values; left-aligned for diagnostic names; center-aligned for status badges.

### 5. Buttons & Form Inputs
- **Primary Button:** Background `#1976D2`, hover `#1565C0`, active `#0D47A1`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius 8px, padding 10px 20px, font weight 600.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Background transparent, border `1px solid #90CAF9`, text `#1976D2`, hover background `#F5F9FD`.
- **Text Inputs:** Height 42px, border `1px solid #D1D5DB`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#1F2937`, focus ring `2px solid #1976D2` with 2px offset.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Primary blue fill when checked with white icon, radius 4px (checkbox) / 9999px (radio).
